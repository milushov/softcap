Softcap — the settings screen

Seven sections, the list on the left. The icons are monochrome line glyphs in the label's colour, the way SF Symbols are in the native macOS sidebars: coloured tiles cheapen the look and argue with the load indicators, where the colour carries meaning. At the foot of the page is a theme switch.

Active in CLI — the token is read directly and never refreshed, otherwise claude would be signed out. Refreshed — the app keeps its own copy of the refresh token, taken while the account was active.
