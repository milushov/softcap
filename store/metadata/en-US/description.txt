Softcap shows how much of your Claude Code and OpenAI Codex subscriptions you have left, in the menu bar, before you run out in the middle of something.

Every subscription limit, on one screen. Softcap reads the five-hour session window and the weekly window for each account you add, and keeps both in front of you: a countdown in the menu bar, a window one click away, widgets on the desktop.

SEVERAL ACCOUNTS AT ONCE
Claude and Codex accounts side by side, as many as you have. You sign in through your own browser, so Softcap never asks for a password and never signs your command line tools out. Hide the accounts you are not watching, and drag the rest into the order you want.

IT TELLS YOU BEFORE YOU HIT IT
Notifications at 80% and 95% out of the box, or whichever levels you set instead — once per crossing, not once per reading. Quiet hours keep the night silent, and nothing is delivered late to make up for it.

A TIMER IN THE MENU BAR
Whichever limit is closest to running out, and how long until it frees up, without clicking anything. The strip can carry the countdown, the percentage, or only the icon.

WIDGETS
All four sizes, drawn from the same account row the window uses, so the two cannot drift apart.

USAGE HISTORY
Weekly-limit charts over the last week or the last month, kept on your Mac and pruned after 35 days.

YOUR TOKENS STAY PUT
Credentials stay in the keychain, never copied into preferences or logs. Checking usage sends no prompts and spends none of your allowance: one request per account per reading, five minutes apart in the background and once a minute while the window is open.

TEN LANGUAGES
Arabic, Bengali, Chinese, English, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian and Spanish — including Arabic laid out right to left. Switching takes effect immediately, with no restart, and VoiceOver reads the whole app.

Free, and open source.
